# Shared formulation for dev-06

The submitted responsive formulation uses theta_a=0.000337 per minute, relative_leak=0.5124966150526034, EC50=140 mg/dL, Hill exponent=4, filtering time constant=2 minutes, and theta_L=4. Each patient's amplitude and reservoir are scaled only by native TDI, as required. No individual coefficients or boluses are used.

The smallest permitted leak and largest permitted Hill exponent provide strong glucose modulation within the allowed material envelope. Rapid filtering limits added sensing lag. The reservoir has a nominal four-TDI load, avoiding deliberate early depletion. The shared design is constrained by child#005's low-glucose excursions; increasing release to benefit the other children can violate this child's safety limits.

The search screened amplitudes and EC50 values, then expanded from the training seed to fresh random-meal seeds with all three meal variants. Initial screening used the public RK4 implementation at one substep per minute for speed. Finalists were evaluated on 70 seeds (630 trajectories); the leader was checked on 100 additional seeds (900 trajectories). The leader at amplitude 0.000340 and EC50=140 achieved 60.47% and 59.96% mean TIR on these respective sets, but had worst TBR70=3.54%. The final amplitude was reduced to 0.000337 to reserve additional safety margin against new meals.

The final design was checked using RK4 at 0.25-minute steps on five selected seeds and all patients/variants (45 trajectories). Mean TIR was 57.34% on this deliberately difficult subset, worst TBR70 was 3.125%, TBR54 was zero, and the full-horizon minimum was 67.37 mg/dL. Native simglucose dopri5 was also checked for child#005, seed 910055, large dinner, the worst observed scenario for the leading design. It returned TIR=80.625%, TBR70=3.125%, TBR54=0%, minimum=67.37056 mg/dL, closely matching the finer RK4 result.

Final combined evaluation of the submitted design covered 170 seeds, three patients, and three variants (1,530 trajectories). Mean post-warmup TIR was 59.12398%; worst trajectory TBR70 was 3.125%; worst TBR54 was 0%; the full-horizon minimum was 67.37035 mg/dL. These aggregate figures use the one-minute RK4 screening integrator; the finer and native checks above are reported separately. The maximum possible three-day release is 0.000337 × (1 + 0.5124966150526034) × 4320 = approximately 2.202 TDI, below the four-TDI reservoir, so depletion cannot occur for this formulation.

All TIR/TBR calculations omit day one; full-horizon minima include it. Seed sets and search stages are recorded in search.py; JSON records retain individual trajectory metrics. The selected-scenario native check does not constitute native validation of every trajectory, and public-seed safety cannot guarantee safety on new meals. This is a simulation benchmark design, not a clinical recommendation.
