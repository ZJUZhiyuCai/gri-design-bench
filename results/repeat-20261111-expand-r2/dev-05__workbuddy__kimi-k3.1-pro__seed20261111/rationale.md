# GRI formulation design

A shared responsive formulation is used. The Hill response raises delivery at
hyperglycaemia while a small load provides basal capacity. Selection targets
mean post-warmup time in range on training scenarios, with every patient and
variant constrained to low TBR and no severe hypoglycaemia. The final candidate
is checked with additional meal seeds to avoid relying on training scenarios.
This is a simulation design, not a clinical recommendation.
